- **Pitch:** Steal a councilman's gold coupe in broad daylight, shake the Port Solana police and decide what a favour is worth.
- **Genre:** action
- **Target platform:** desktop browsers (WebGL2), touch fallback controls on mobile

## 4. Story, characters and missions
- **Premise:** A broke courier who swore off crime must steal a councilman's gold coupe for the fixer who saved his life, and decide whether a favour repaid is worth more than a fair price.
- **Characters:**
  - **Dez Varga** (dez) — protagonist; wants: Pay back Lu and walk away clean.
  - **Lu Anwen** (lu) — fixer; wants: Embarrass Councilman Pryce before the election.
  - **Tomas Ruiz** (ruiz) — mechanic; wants: Retire to his brother's farm with clean hands.
- **Quests / missions:**
  - Story script: Borrowed Shine — review with `gameforge story <file.story.js>` (graph + story bible)
  - **Borrowed Shine** — Lu asks Dez to steal Councilman Pryce's gold coupe, shake the police and bring it to the Halfmoon body shop. Objectives: Steal the gold coupe; Lose the police; Bring the coupe to Halfmoon; Hand the coupe to Ruiz
  - **Payday** — Dez returns to Lu; how she settles depends on whether he kept his word. Objectives: Settle up with Lu

- **Authored asset scripts (review with gameforge model/anim/sound/scene/cinematic):** `assets/halfmoon-garage.model.js`

## 8. Technical architecture
- GameForge engine (vendored three.js r180 renderer, in-house physics/AI/audio/UI), no install, no build step for development
- Modules: `src/world.js`, `src/assets.js`, `src/story.js`, `src/gameplay.js`, `src/ai.js`, `src/audio.js`, `src/hud.js`, `src/animation.js`, `src/graphics.js`; composed by `main.js` via `composeGame(SPEC)`

## 9. Asset requirements
All assets are generated procedurally at load time (no files, no paid generators): terrain, vegetation, buildings, props, characters, animation clips, textures, SFX and music.


## 10. Performance targets
- 60 fps on a mid-range discrete/integrated GPU at 1080p on the "medium" tier; quality tiers auto-detected (low/medium/high)
- Draw calls < 300, triangles < 1.5M on high; shadow map ≤ 4096
- Note: headless CI uses software WebGL (SwiftShader) and is expected to run far below these targets; it verifies correctness, not speed
