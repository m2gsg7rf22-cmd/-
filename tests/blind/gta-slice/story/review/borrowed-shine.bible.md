# Borrowed Shine — Story Bible

> Generated by `gameforge story` from the story script. Design document; gameplay proof comes from playtests.

**Premise.** A broke courier who swore off crime must steal a councilman's gold coupe for the fixer who saved his life, and decide whether a favour repaid is worth more than a fair price.

**Logline.** One car, one favour, one city that never forgets who drove it.

**Themes.** Is a debt of gratitude ever paid in full? · Who owns a favour once it is cashed?

**Tone.** Sun-bleached street crime; quick humour; consequences that land in daylight.

## World

Port Solana, a hot harbour city of palm-lined avenues; this slice is Cobalt Row, a dense mixed district of shops and towers.

- Cobalt Row runs on cash, scooters and favours owed to Lu Anwen.
- Councilman Pryce parks his gold coupe on the street because nobody would dare touch it.
- The Halfmoon body shop repaints anything in forty minutes, no questions.
- Port Solana police answer gunfire fast and car theft faster on Cobalt Row.

  - Fresh primer drips on the kerb
  - A licence-plate stack sorted by colour
  - Ruiz's radio playing old ballads

## Structure

### Act I: The Favour

_Setup and payoff in one district: the job, the theft, the heat, the choice._

#### Borrowed Shine (`borrowed_shine`)

Lu asks Dez to steal Councilman Pryce's gold coupe, shake the police and bring it to the Halfmoon body shop.

Giver: lu · requires: — · 11 lines, 3 choice points, 11 words/line

Beats:
1. Lu calls in the favour over tacos.
2. Dez breaks into the gold coupe in broad daylight.
3. Witnesses call it in; police swarm Cobalt Row.
4. Dez loses the heat and reaches Halfmoon.
5. Dez decides: keep his word or squeeze Lu for a fair price.

Objectives:
- [custom steal_car] Steal the gold coupe
- [custom lose_heat] Lose the police _(after steal)_
- [reach halfmoon] Bring the coupe to Halfmoon _(after heat)_
- [flag coupe_handed] Hand the coupe to Ruiz _(after drop)_

Branches:
- **word** sets `kept_word` → Lu calls the debt paid and treats Dez as a partner at payday. _(folds back at payday)_
- **squeeze** sets `squeezed_lu` → Lu pays in cash but cuts Dez out of future jobs. _(folds back at payday)_

#### Payday (`payday`)

Dez returns to Lu; how she settles depends on whether he kept his word.

Giver: lu · requires: borrowed_shine · starts automatically · 4 lines, 2 choice points, 10 words/line

Beats:
1. Lu has already heard from Ruiz.
2. She settles the debt her way, and Dez learns what kind of favour it was.

Objectives:
- [flag settled] Settle up with Lu
