# The Blue Ledger — Story Bible

> Generated by `gameforge story` from the story script. Design document; gameplay proof comes from playtests.

**Premise.** A getaway driver who works alone must join a crew to steal a crime boss's bribe ledger — and decide whether the truth inside it is worth more than her brother's debt.

**Logline.** In a drowning port city, the only way to pay a debt is to steal the book it is written in.

**Themes.** Loyalty you are paid for versus loyalty you choose · Truth has a price, and someone else usually pays it

**Tone.** Rain-noir with dry humour; grounded violence; warm crew banter against a cold city.

## World

Harrowgate, a tidal port of container cranes, flooded lower streets and neon fish markets, run by one shipping magnate.

- Harrowgate floods at spring tide; the lower districts live on stilts and gangways.
- Augustin Crane owns the container terminal, the tug fleet and three judges.
- Debt markers are written on blue paper; owing Crane is called "wearing blue".
- The Lantern Regatta: once a year every boat carries a paper lantern; fireworks start at nine.
- The Customs House vault predates electricity; its dial was made by Teo's old teacher.

### Locations and environmental storytelling hooks (for the World Builder)

- **Kettle Street** (`kettle_street`, village at south) — Noodle stalls under tarps, steam and rain.
  - Ines's car parked under a broken awning, blanket on the back seat
  - Blue debt markers pinned to a stall's "wall of shame"
  - A noodle stall with two stools, one always kept for Teo
- **Crane Yards** (`crane_yards`, camp at east) — Container stacks, sodium lights, a yard office on stilts.
  - Containers stencilled with Crane's heron logo
  - A guard rota chalked on a whiteboard
  - Tyre marks where the last debtor tried to drive away
- **Saltline Relay** (`relay_tower`, tower at north) — A rusting radio mast Pell has turned into a home.
  - Cables strung like laundry
  - Pell's hand-painted sign: "nothing here works (on purpose)"
  - Relay chips glinting in puddles below the mast
- **Customs House** (`customs_vault`, ruins at west) — A grand old customs hall, half archive, half fortress.
  - Parcels stamped twice: once by customs, once by Crane
  - Odile's window with a child's drawing taped inside
  - Service door with fresh oil on its hinges
- **Harbor Mouth** (`harbor_mouth`, camp at southwest) — Where the lantern boats leave the harbor; the escape route.
  - Lantern boats bumping hulls
  - A drawbridge operator asleep in his booth
- **Crane Tower** (`crane_tower`, tower at northeast) — Glass office above the terminal; Crane's view of everything he owns.
  - A heron sculpture made of melted debt-marker pins
  - Floor-to-ceiling window overlooking the flooded districts

## Structure

### Act I — Wearing Blue

_Setup: the debt, the job, the crew. Ends when Ines commits to a crew (ghost_wire + collectors)._

#### Cold Engine (`cold_engine`)

Teo pitches the Customs House job. Scout the yards and steal the vault blueprints.

Giver: teo · requires: — · 11 lines, 1 choice points, 11.5 words/line

Beats:
1. Rain on Kettle Street; Ines sleeps in her car.
2. Teo pitches the ledger job (inciting incident).
3. Ines chooses her motive: Mika or money.
4. Scout Crane Yards, lift the blueprints.
5. Teo names the third member: Pell.

Objectives:
- [reach crane_yards] Scout the Crane Yards
- [collect blueprint] Steal the vault blueprints _(after scout)_
- [talk teo] Bring the blueprints to Teo _(after plans)_

Branches:
- **family** sets `motive_family` → Crane leverages Mika in the finale; Mika thanks her in the epilogue. _(folds back at ledger_weight)_
- **money** sets `motive_money` → Crane tries to outbid the ledger; Mika hears she did it for money. _(folds back at ledger_weight)_

#### Ghost in the Wire (`ghost_wire`)

Recruit Pell, who wants the ledger made public. Gather relay chips to own the grid.

Giver: pell · requires: cold_engine · 8 lines, 1 choice points, 10.9 words/line

Beats:
1. Ines climbs the Saltline Relay.
2. Pell sets a condition: the ledger goes public.
3. Ines promises — or bargains.
4. Relay chips gathered; the cameras are theirs.

Objectives:
- [collect relay_chip ×3] Gather relay chips
- [talk pell] Bring the chips to Pell _(after chips)_

Branches:
- **leak** sets `promised_leak` → Pell trusts Ines; selling the ledger later breaks that promise on screen. _(folds back at ledger_weight)_
- **sale** sets `promised_sale` → Pell stays wary through the regatta. _(folds back at ledger_weight)_

#### Collectors (`collectors`)

Crane's enforcer comes for Mika's debt. Pay him off or fight.

Giver: bram · requires: cold_engine · 4 lines, 1 choice points, 7.3 words/line

Beats:
1. Bram corners Ines in the yards and starts counting.
2. Ines pays with the heist advance — or makes it loud.

Objectives:
- [flag bram_answered] Answer Bram

Branches:
- **pay** sets `bram_pay` → Ines loses her advance; Crane later respects her as "a person who pays". _(folds back at inside_man)_
- **fight** sets `bram_fight` → Crane doubles the guards on regatta night. _(folds back at inside_man)_

### Act II — The Inside

_Confrontation: the debt is handled one way or another (foldback), the crew needs a way in._

#### Quiet Money (`quiet_money`) — optional

Bring Bram the cash.

Giver: bram · requires: collectors · flags: bram_pay · 2 lines, 1 choice points, 11 words/line

Beats:
1. Ines empties her stash.
2. Bram counts it, exactly.

Objectives:
- [collect cash_bundle ×2] Collect the cash bundles
- [talk bram] Pay Bram _(after cash)_

#### The Hard Way (`hard_way`) — optional

Put Bram's crew on the ground.

Giver: bram · requires: collectors · flags: bram_fight · 2 lines, 1 choice points, 7 words/line

Beats:
1. Three collectors close in among the containers.
2. Bram watches, still counting.

Objectives:
- [kill thug ×3] Beat Bram's collectors
- [talk bram] Face Bram _(after thugs)_

#### The Inside Man (`inside_man`)

Turn Crane's customs clerk: threaten her or offer her a way out.

Giver: odile · requires: ghost_wire, collectors · flags: collectors_handled · 8 lines, 1 choice points, 12.1 words/line

Beats:
1. Ines queues at the customs window without a parcel.
2. Odile reveals Crane holds her daughter's papers.
3. Threat or promise.
4. Ines cases the Customs House.

Objectives:
- [reach customs_vault] Case the Customs House
- [talk odile] Collect the visitor pass from Odile _(after case)_

Branches:
- **trust** sets `marrow_trusted` → Odile opens the service door warmly; unlocks the witness ending. _(folds back at regatta)_
- **threat** sets `marrow_blackmailed` → Teo worries she will make a call; the witness ending is closed. _(folds back at regatta)_

### Act III — Lanterns

_Climax and resolution: the heist, the decision, the cost._

#### Lantern Regatta (`regatta`)

Fireworks at nine. Steal the ledger and get out through the harbor mouth.

Giver: teo · requires: inside_man · 11 lines, 2 choice points, 11.4 words/line

Beats:
1. Teo briefs the crew; his hands shake.
2. Cameras die for six minutes.
3. Ines takes the dial; Teo takes the wheel (midpoint reversal of roles).
4. Guards, ledger, escape through the lantern boats.

Objectives:
- [reach customs_vault] Get into the Customs House
- [collect ledger] Crack the vault and take the ledger _(after in)_
- [kill grunt ×2] Deal with the vault guards _(after in)_
- [reach harbor_mouth] Escape through the harbor mouth _(after book, guards)_

#### What the Ledger Weighs (`ledger_weight`)

Crane summons Ines to his tower. Leak the ledger, sell it, or hand it to a witness.

Giver: crane · requires: regatta · starts automatically · 12 lines, 1 choice points, 10.3 words/line

Beats:
1. Crane's invitation arrives on blue paper.
2. Crane offers her exactly what she wanted (the dark night of the soul).
3. Ines decides what the truth is for.

Objectives:
- [reach crane_tower] Go up Crane Tower
- [flag ledger_decided] Decide the ledger's fate _(after tower)_

Branches:
- **leak** sets `ending_leak` → The city reads everything; Mika's debt stays, Crane falls. _(folds back at low_tide)_
- **sell** sets `ending_sale` → Mika walks free; Crane keeps the port; Pell is betrayed if promised. _(folds back at low_tide)_
- **witness** sets `ending_witness` → Odile testifies; slow justice, everyone pays a little. _(folds back at low_tide)_

#### Low Tide (`low_tide`)

Epilogue on Kettle Street: Mika, the morning after.

Giver: mika · requires: ledger_weight · starts automatically · 5 lines, 1 choice points, 13.4 words/line

Beats:
1. Morning after the regatta; the tide is out.
2. Mika tells Ines what the city is saying (the consequence montage).
3. Ines decides whether he gets in the car (the arc lands).

Objectives:
- [talk mika] Find Mika on Kettle Street
